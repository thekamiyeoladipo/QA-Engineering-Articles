Every great software starts with a plan. The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is like the blueprint for building any software. It’s a series of phases that include planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance. But where does QA then fit in? QA engineers play a crucial throughout the SDLC, ensuring the quality is embedded at every phase. In the early stages, you might be involved in planning and gathering requirements. During development, you’ll be testing and identifying bugs. After the software is deployed, your role shifts to monitoring and supporting to make sure users aren’t tunning into issues. The SDLC gives you a roadmap for your testing process and lets you know when to jump in. It’s a framework that helps keep everything on track and ensures the final product is top-notch
