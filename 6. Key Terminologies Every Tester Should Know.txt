Welcome to the world acronyms and jargon! In the QA industry, there are tons of terms that you’ll need to know to communicate effectively with your team. Let’s start with the basics: test cases, test scripts, defects and user stories. A test case is a detailed document that describes the conditions and steps to test a specific feature. A test script is similar but often automated, guiding the system to tun specific tests. Defects or bugs are issues found during testing, and they are logged in a defect tracking system for the dev team to fix. User stories are short descriptions of a feature from the user’s perspective, which helps you understand the purpose of the feature you’re testing. There are countless other jargon in the QA industry, however familiarizing yourself with these terms will make your journey smoother.
