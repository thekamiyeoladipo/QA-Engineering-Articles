Most people think QA engineers just just do a few tasks in the organzation, the role of QA Engineer however is multifaceted. You are not just a tester; you are an important part of the product development team. A QA Engineer ensures that a product meets both the functional requirements and the end-users' expectations. This involves designing test cases, executing tests, logging bugs, and working with developers to fix issues. QA is about finding problems before they reach the user, ensuring a smooth, high-quality user experience, and maintaining software stabbility at all stages of its development.
